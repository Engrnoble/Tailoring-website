How It Works

From fabric to fit, in three steps

01

Book or visit

Call, WhatsApp or walk into the studio Monday to Friday, 8am–5pm, and tell us what you need — a new piece, an alteration, or a repair.

02

We take precise measurements, talk through fabric and style, and schedule any follow-up fittings needed to get the fit exactly right.

Pickup or delivery
